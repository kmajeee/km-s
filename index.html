Peer-Reviewed Research Paper

AI-Powered Smart Shopping Trolley — Design and Implementation

Sheik Abdul Majeeth K M • Francis Xavier Engineering College

Abstract: This research paper details the end-to-end design, embedded hardware architecture, and software orchestration of an intelligent retail trolley built with Flutter, Firebase, NFC, IoT microcontrollers, and BLE communication. The system tackles major bottlenecks in retail shopping: long billing queues, customer friction, cart misplacement, and inventory shrinkage.

Automated Billing
NFC & RFID barcode scanning paired with continuous weight telemetry to register purchases instantly.
Anti-Theft Security
HX711 load cell sensor verification detects un-scanned items or weight mismatches immediately.
AI Product Engine
Real-time cart-based recommendation system suggesting complimentary products based on customer habits.
Child Safety Geofence
BLE RSSI beacon triangulation preventing carts from traversing out of bounds with automated wheel locks.
